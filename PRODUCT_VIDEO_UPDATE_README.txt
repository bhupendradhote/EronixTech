PRODUCT VIDEO SUPPORT ADDED

1. Run add_product_video_support.sql once in phpMyAdmin/MySQL for an existing database.
2. Backend supports one uploaded video (MP4/WebM/OGG/MOV, max 120 MB) or one YouTube URL per product.
3. Admin: Products > Add/Edit Product > Product Video.
4. Product page shows a Video thumbnail only when a video is configured.
5. Restart backend and rebuild frontend after deployment.
